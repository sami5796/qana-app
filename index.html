Meet a word. Find its letters. Hear a story.

Qana: Tigrinya for Kids gives children short Tigrinya adventures, games, letter tracing and picture books. It is made for ages 6–8; younger children can play with a grown-up beside them. The app is in English or Norwegian, and Tigrinya is what children learn.

The picture books are read aloud in Tigrinya by a real narrator. Qana never uses the microphone.

Five places to feel at home.

Short missions, playful practice and room to try again.

01

Adventure

Eight worlds and 32 short missions. Each mission brings three Tigrinya words or phrases to recognise, read and match with their meanings.

02

Games

Word pairs, Word detective, Bubble pop and Feed the bird, each with five levels and stars to earn, plus Listen & find.

03

Letters

All 32 fidel letter families, seven forms each. Trace them with a finger or Apple Pencil, then try the letter quiz.

04

Stories

Three picture books (My family, Food and The weather) read aloud in Tigrinya by a human narrator, with a picture game.

05

Treasures

Stickers, medals, flowers and the words found so far: a place to see learning grow.

Built around family life

Learning here.
Choices with you.

Everything stays on the device. Qana has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it sends nothing to us.

Settings, profiles, backups, reminders, purchases and every link out of the app sit behind a parent gate. In the grown-ups’ area you can export a backup, remove a profile or erase all Qana data.

The picture books and a few example sentences use published recordings of a human Tigrinya narrator from LIDA Stories. The Tigrinya words and most example sentences are spoken from clips inside the app, made in advance with an AI voice. Letters have no recordings, and Qana does not score pronunciation.

Litt tigrinja, hver dag.

Qana: Tigrinja for barn er laget for barn fra 6 til 8 år. Yngre barn kan spille sammen med en voksen. Appen har fem deler: Eventyr, Spill, Bokstaver, Historier og Skatter. De tre bildebøkene leses opp på tigrinja av en ekte forteller. De to første verdenene, alle bokstaver og all sporing, alle spill og alle tre historier er gratis. Qana Family er et engangskjøp som åpner de seks andre verdenene og gir plass til opptil seks elevprofiler. Alt lagres på enheten.

What Qana stores

Qana stores learner profile names, learning progress, activity results and app preferences on your device. A nickname is enough; you do not need to provide a child's full name, date of birth, email address or school.

There are no Qana accounts, advertising services, analytics tools or cloud AI. Qana has no backend that receives learner profiles or progress. It does not upload children's activity to the developer.

The app teaches reading, recognition and writing. It has no audio lessons or pronunciation assessment and does not need a recording of your child's voice.

Device backups and files you choose to share

On-device storage does not mean data can never leave the device. Your system backups, including Apple iCloud Backup, may include Qana's app data depending on your device settings. Apple manages those backups; Qana does not operate its own cloud sync or backup service.

Exporting a backup in the parent area creates a file containing learner profiles and progress. You choose where to save or share it through Apple's file and sharing controls. If you choose iCloud Drive, another storage provider or a messaging app, that service handles the file under its own terms.

Importing reads a backup you choose into Qana's local storage. Review the app's confirmation before importing: an import can replace existing progress. Keep a separate backup if you want to preserve the current state.

Purchases are handled by Apple

Qana Family is a one-time in-app purchase. Apple's App Store and StoreKit handle the payment and provide verified transaction information so Qana can determine access on your device. Restoring purchases also communicates with Apple.

The developer does not receive your card details or Apple Account password. Apple's handling of purchase information is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Qana does not send transaction information to a developer-operated server.

Optional local reminders

If you enable reminders and allow notifications, Qana schedules them locally on your device. They do not require a push-notification backend. Turn them off in the app or in your device's notification settings. Notification previews may be visible on the lock screen according to your settings.

Children and the parent gate

Qana's main audience is children aged 6–8. Children who cannot yet read need a grown-up beside them. Profile management, backup export and import, purchases and external links in the app are behind a parent gate.

A math challenge is a practical barrier before an adult action. It does not verify a person's identity or age, and it is not legal consent to collect or share a child's personal information.

Qana does not have child accounts, public profiles, chat or a place for children to upload content. The support channel below is for adults only.

This website and adult support

This public website contains no analytics scripts, advertising, embedded social widgets or externally hosted fonts. Its artwork and stylesheet are served with the pages. There is no Qana sign-in or contact form.

GitHub hosts the website and the public support issue tracker. Visiting the site makes network requests to GitHub; GitHub may process connection information such as an IP address and browser details under the GitHub privacy statement. A website visit is separate from a child's local learning activity.

If an adult opens a support issue, their GitHub username and the information they post are visible to the developer and the public. We use that information to respond to questions, investigate problems and improve Qana. Issue discussions may remain visible, including in GitHub history or copies made by others.

Do not post a child's name, photo, voice, school, location, progress file or other identifying information. Do not attach receipts, payment details, account identifiers or unredacted screenshots. A short description of the problem is usually enough.

Removing data and asking questions

Deleting the app removes its local data from that device. Offloading the app can retain its documents and data. Exported files, system backups and public support posts are separate copies; manage those in the relevant Apple, storage-provider or GitHub settings.

For a privacy question, contact Qana's developer through the adult support channel. Describe the concern without posting private information or a child's details. For a public support post, you can edit your post and ask the maintainer about removal; this cannot guarantee removal of copies held by others.

We will update this page and its date if Qana's data practices change.

Norsk sammendrag

Qana lagrer elevprofiler, fremgang og innstillinger på enheten. Appen har ingen kontoer, reklame, analyseverktøy eller skybasert KI, og sender ikke barnets læringsaktivitet til utvikleren.

Enhetskopier, blant annet iCloud-sikkerhetskopi, kan inneholde appdata. Hvis en voksen eksporterer en sikkerhetskopi, bestemmer den voksne hvor filen lagres eller deles. Kjøp og gjenoppretting av kjøp behandles av Apple. Påminnelser er valgfrie og lokale.

GitHub er vert for dette nettstedet og den offentlige brukerstøtten. Støttehenvendelser er bare for voksne. Ikke legg ut opplysninger om barn, sikkerhetskopier eller betalingsinformasjon. Regneoppgaven i foreldreporten er ikke et juridisk samtykke til datainnsamling.

The short version. Everything Qana saves stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking, and the app sends nothing to us. We do not collect any personal information from children.

Who we are

Qana: Tigrinya for Kids (in Norwegian, Qana: Tigrinja for barn) is made by Samuel Tekie Teklemariam, based in Norway. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Samuel Tekie Teklemariam. We are responsible for the small amount of personal information we may handle, which in practice is an email you choose to send us.

Questions about privacy, or anything on this page: samueltekie57@gmail.com.

What Qana stores, and where

Qana saves the following in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • Each learner’s nickname. It is optional; if you leave it out, Qana simply uses “Explorer”. Qana never asks for a child’s full name, birthday, photo, email address or school.
  • Lesson and game progress: stars, stickers, treasures and game levels.
  • Saved words.
  • A day-by-day practice log (how much was practised each day), kept for up to 400 days.
  • App preferences, such as the app language, sounds and reminder time.

To protect progress if a file is ever damaged, Qana also keeps internal safety copies of its progress file on the same device so it can recover. These copies never leave the device.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Qana works without an account and without an internet connection.

What we don’t collect

We collect no data through the app. In Apple’s App Store terms, this is Data Not Collected.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • No analytics or usage statistics.
  • No third-party SDKs and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No network requests of Qana’s own. The only connection is Apple’s App Store, when a grown-up buys or restores Qana Family.
  • No access to the microphone, camera, contacts, photos or location. Qana never asks for them.

Children’s privacy

Qana is made for children aged 6–8, and younger children can play with a grown-up. We do not collect personal information from children. A child’s nickname and progress are stored only on the device and never leave it through Qana.

For that reason we do not request parental consent under the US Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) or Article 8 of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). If that ever changes, we will update this policy first and obtain verifiable parental consent before any personal information is collected from a child.

Qana has no chat, no public profiles and nowhere for a child to post or share anything.

The parent gate

Settings, profiles, backups, reminders, purchases and every link out of the app are protected by a parent gate: a multiplication question with two two-digit numbers, different each time.

The parent gate is a practical safeguard that keeps young children out of grown-up areas. It does not check anyone’s age or identity, and it is not age verification or legal parental consent.

Sound and the microphone

  • The three picture books in Stories, and a few example sentences, use published recordings of a human Tigrinya narrator from LIDA Stories, shared under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (CC BY 4.0). Each book credits its author, illustrator, translator and narrator. LIDA Stories does not endorse Qana.
  • Task instructions can be read aloud in English or Norwegian by your device’s built-in voice.
  • Soft sound effects can be turned off in the grown-ups’ area.
  • Single words and letters have no recordings.

Qana never uses the microphone, never records a child and does not score pronunciation.

Purchases are handled by Apple

Qana Family is a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase with no subscription. Apple’s App Store handles the payment, restores and Family Sharing. Qana only checks Apple’s confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Apple Account, and that check happens on the device.

Qana never sees your payment details or Apple Account password, and we receive no purchase information from the app. Apple’s handling of purchases is covered by Apple’s privacy policy.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and off until a grown-up turns them on in the grown-ups’ area. They are scheduled on the device, with no server involved. A reminder shows the word of the day, never a child’s name. Depending on your settings, notification previews may be visible on the lock screen. You can turn reminders off in Qana or in your device’s notification settings.

Widgets

Qana’s two widgets, Word of the day and Letter of the day, show course content that comes with the app. They cannot read learner names or progress.

Backups and copies you control

In the grown-ups’ area, a grown-up can export a backup. This creates a file containing learner nicknames, progress and preferences, saved to Files or wherever you choose. You decide where it goes and who you share it with. If you save it to iCloud Drive or another storage service, that service holds the file under its own terms.

Restoring a backup replaces all current learner profiles and preferences on the device with the ones in the file. Export the current learning first if you want to keep it.

Separately, your device’s own iCloud Backup or computer backups may include Qana’s data, according to the device owner’s settings. Apple and you manage those backups, not us.

How to delete data

Everything Qana stores is on your device, so you delete it there. Open the grown-ups’ area (Family space) and choose:

  • Remove a profile: deletes that learner’s progress, stars and stickers from the device, including from Qana’s internal recovery copy. This can’t be undone.
  • Erase all Qana data: deletes every learner, all progress and all settings on the device, and Qana starts again from the beginning. A Qana Family purchase stays with your Apple Account and can be restored. Exported backups in Files are not touched.

Deleting the app also removes all of its data from that device. (Offloading the app in iOS settings keeps its data.)

Exported backup files and iCloud or computer device backups are separate copies that your family controls. Delete those in Files, iCloud Drive or your Apple settings.

If you have emailed us and want that message deleted, write to samueltekie57@gmail.com.

How long data is kept

  • The practice log keeps up to 400 days; the oldest days are removed automatically.
  • Everything else stays on the device until you remove a profile, erase all Qana data or delete the app.
  • Support emails are deleted within 12 months of our last reply.

Third parties

  • In the app: none. Qana contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, advertising or tracking services. The LIDA Stories recordings are included in the app, so Qana does not contact LIDA Stories.
  • Apple handles purchases, restores and Family Sharing, and any iCloud or device backups you choose to make, under Apple’s privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may pass crash reports to us. Apple controls this, and you can switch it off there.
  • GitHub hosts this website and the optional public issue tracker, under the GitHub privacy statement.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

This website, and when you contact us

This website has no analytics, advertising, scripts, embedded social widgets or externally hosted fonts. Its pages, artwork and stylesheet are served by GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process connection details such as your IP address and browser. A website visit is separate from anything that happens in the app.

Email

If you email samueltekie57@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you, and we delete the conversation within 12 months of our last reply. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering your message (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). Please leave out your child’s name and other details; a short description of the question is enough.

GitHub issues (optional)

You can also ask a question in Qana’s public GitHub issue tracker. It needs a GitHub account, and everything posted there, including your GitHub username, is public. Please never post a child’s name or details, a backup file, receipts or payment information.

Your rights

If you live in Norway, the EU or the EEA, you have the right to access, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, such as a support email, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Because app data never reaches us, we cannot see it or delete it for you; you control it on the device as described above.

To use your rights, write to samueltekie57@gmail.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle personal data, you can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority, Datatilsynet (www.datatilsynet.no), or to the data protection authority where you live.

Changes to this policy

If Qana’s data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date. We will not start collecting personal information from children without first updating this policy and obtaining verifiable parental consent.

Effective 21 October 2026

Norsk sammendrag

Qana: Tigrinja for barn er laget av Samuel Tekie Teklemariam, Norge. Spørsmål om personvern kan sendes til samueltekie57@gmail.com.

Alt Qana lagrer, blir på enheten: kallenavnet til hver elev (valgfritt, ellers brukes «Utforsker»), fremgang i oppdrag og spill, lagrede ord, en daglig øvingslogg (opptil 400 dager) og innstillinger. Appen har ingen kontoer, ingen reklame, ingen analyseverktøy, ingen tredjeparts-SDK-er og ingen sporing, og den sender ingenting til oss. Vi samler ikke inn personopplysninger fra barn, og derfor ber vi ikke om foreldresamtykke etter COPPA eller GDPR artikkel 8. Hvis dette endres, oppdaterer vi personvernerklæringen først og innhenter verifiserbart samtykke fra foreldrene før noe samles inn.

Bildebøkene i Historier bruker publiserte opptak fra LIDA Stories, lest inn av en ekte forteller. Qana bruker aldri mikrofonen og vurderer ikke uttale. Kjøp av Qana Family håndteres av Apple. Påminnelser er valgfrie, planlegges på enheten og viser dagens ord, aldri barnets navn. Widgetene viser bare innhold som følger med appen.

Foreldreporten (et gangestykke) er en praktisk sperre, ikke alderskontroll eller juridisk samtykke. I Familieområdet kan en voksen eksportere en sikkerhetskopi, fjerne en profil eller slette alle Qana-data. Sletter du appen, forsvinner også dataene på enheten. Eksporterte sikkerhetskopier og iCloud-sikkerhetskopier er egne kopier som familien selv styrer.

Du kan be om innsyn, retting eller sletting av opplysninger vi har om deg, for eksempel en e-post. Du kan også klage til Datatilsynet. E-post til brukerstøtten slettes senest 12 måneder etter siste svar. Les hjelpesiden på norsk.

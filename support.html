Start small, together

Qana is designed primarily for children aged 6–8. A child who is not reading yet needs a grown-up to help with instructions and meanings.

Adventure has eight worlds and 32 short, three-word missions. Play offers practice games, Letters introduces the Fidel script and tracing, and My garden shows learning progress. The missions draw on 92 vocabulary items; familiar items can appear again.

Why is there no sound?

Qana teaches reading, recognition and writing. It does not include recorded speech, listening exercises or pronunciation feedback. Read alongside your child, and use a Tigrinya-speaking family member or teacher for spoken-language guidance.

What is free?

The first two worlds, all letters, tracing and games are free. You can keep using them without purchasing Qana Family or creating an account.

Qana Family, once

Family unlocks the remaining worlds and up to six learner profiles on one device. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription. The US price is configured at $24.99 in App Store Connect. Qana is still in preparation and is not released. When available, the app will show Apple's localized price before purchase.

Restore an existing purchase

  1. Check that the device is using the Apple Account that made the purchase.
  2. Open Qana's parent area and complete the parent gate.
  3. Open the Qana Family purchase screen and choose Restore purchases.

An internet connection may be needed to contact Apple. Restoring a purchase restores access, not learning progress from another device. If nothing is found, check the Apple Account and whether the purchase was completed or refunded.

A request is waiting for approval

If Apple Ask to Buy is enabled, a parent or guardian must approve the request through Apple. Waiting is not a completed purchase. Check the original request before using an explicit retry in the parent area; avoid submitting repeated requests.

The price will not load

Check your connection and try again later from the parent area. Qana does not invent a replacement price. Free worlds, letters, tracing and games remain available.

Request a refund

Apple handles refund requests and determines eligibility. An adult can request a refund from Apple or read Apple's refund instructions. Do not post a receipt or payment information in a public support issue.

Keep progress safe

Qana keeps learner profiles and progress on the device. There is no Qana account, backend or cloud AI, and no Qana service that synchronizes learning between devices.

Use the parent area's export action to save a backup in a place you control. Before importing another backup, export the current progress and read the import confirmation: the incoming file may replace it. Do not upload a backup to the public support tracker.

Apple's device backups, including iCloud Backup, may also include Qana data depending on your settings. Deleting the app removes local data; exported files and device backups need to be managed separately. Read how backups affect privacy.

Choose whether to be reminded

Reminders are optional local notifications. If you want them, allow notifications when prompted and choose your reminder settings in the app. If they do not appear, check notification permissions and Focus settings on the device. You can switch them off in the app or device settings.

Contact the developer

For adults only. Support issues are public.

Please leave out children's names, photos, voices, schools, locations and progress files. Do not include payment information, Apple Account details or screenshots with personal information.

Use the public Qana support repository to ask a question, report a problem or suggest a language correction. It is a support site, not a place to upload learner data.

Open an adult support issue ↗

This opens GitHub. Posting there requires a GitHub account; using Qana does not.

What helps us understand a problem?

  • The Qana version and the iOS or iPadOS version.
  • A general device model, such as “iPad,” without its serial number.
  • The screen or mission, what you tried and what happened instead.
  • For a language correction: the displayed word or phrase and your suggested correction. No information about your child is needed.

Please type a short description instead of attaching a backup. If you include a screenshot, remove all identifying information first. Do not send sensitive material to demonstrate the problem.

For questions about data handling, start with the privacy policy and describe any remaining concern in an adult support issue without private details.

Hjelp på norsk

Qana er laget for barn fra 6 til 8 år. Barn som ikke leser ennå, trenger en voksen ved siden av seg. Appen øver på lesing, gjenkjenning og skriving og har ikke lyd eller uttaletrening.

De to første verdenene, alle bokstaver, tegneøvelser og spill er gratis. Qana Family er et engangskjøp som åpner resten av verdenene og gir plass til opptil seks elevprofiler på én enhet. Ingen abonnement. Den lokale prisen vises av Apple før kjøpet.

Et tidligere kjøp kan gjenopprettes fra Qana Family-skjermen i foreldreområdet. Bruk Apple-kontoen som kjøpet ble gjort med. Gjenoppretting av kjøpet henter ikke fremgang fra en annen enhet. Refusjon håndteres av Apple.

Fremgangen lagres på enheten. En voksen kan eksportere og importere sikkerhetskopier; en import kan erstatte eksisterende fremgang. Enhetskopier i iCloud kan inneholde appdata, avhengig av innstillingene dine.

Brukerstøtten på GitHub er offentlig og bare for voksne. Du kan skrive på norsk. Ikke del opplysninger om barn, sikkerhetskopier, kvitteringer eller betalingsinformasjon. Beskriv problemet, appversjonen og hva du prøvde.

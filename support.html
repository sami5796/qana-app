What is Qana?

Qana: Tigrinya for Kids (in Norwegian, Qana: Tigrinja for barn) helps children discover Eritrean Tigrinya at their own pace. It is made for children aged 6–8; younger children can play with a grown-up beside them. It runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later.

The app’s interface is in English or Norwegian (Bokmål), and Tigrinya is what children learn: the fidel letters and everyday words, a few at a time.

The five places

  • Adventure: eight worlds with 32 short missions. Each mission brings three Tigrinya words or phrases, with 92 different ones in all.
  • Games: Word pairs, Word detective, Bubble pop and Feed the bird, each with five levels and stars to earn, plus Listen & find, a listening picture game.
  • Letters: all 32 fidel letter families, seven forms each. Children trace them with a finger or Apple Pencil, and there is a letter quiz.
  • Stories: three picture books (My family, Food and The weather) read aloud in Tigrinya, with a picture game.
  • Treasures: stickers, medals, flowers and the words found so far.

In Norwegian the tabs are called Eventyr, Spill, Bokstaver, Historier and Skatter.

What can my child hear?

  • Stories: the three picture books, and a few example sentences, use published recordings of a human Tigrinya narrator from LIDA Stories (CC BY 4.0). Each book credits its author, illustrator, translator and narrator. LIDA Stories does not endorse Qana.
  • Instructions: task instructions can be read aloud in English or Norwegian by your device’s built-in voice.
  • Sound effects: soft sound effects can be turned off in the grown-ups’ area.

Single words and letters have no recordings, so they are silent. Qana never uses the microphone, never records your child and does not score pronunciation. For spoken Tigrinya, a Tigrinya-speaking family member or teacher is the best guide.

What’s free, and what does Qana Family add?

Free: the first two worlds, all letters and tracing, all games and all three stories. You can keep using them without buying anything or creating an account.

Qana Family is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription. It opens the other six worlds and allows up to six learner profiles on the device. Family Sharing is supported. Apple shows the price in your local currency before you buy.

Buying and restoring Qana Family

Buy

  1. Open the grown-ups’ area, Family space (see how to open it).
  2. Choose See Qana Family.
  3. Check the price Apple shows, then confirm the purchase with Apple.

Restore an earlier purchase

  1. Make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase, or one in the same Family Sharing group.
  2. In Family space, or on the Qana Family screen, choose Restore purchases.

Restoring needs an internet connection to reach the App Store. It restores access to Qana Family, not learning progress from another device; use a backup for that.

Family Sharing

If the purchaser has Family Sharing turned on, members of the same family group can use Qana Family on their own devices. Use Restore purchases if it doesn’t appear by itself.

A request is waiting for approval

If Ask to Buy is turned on, Qana shows that the purchase is waiting. Qana Family opens once Apple confirms the approved purchase.

The price won’t load

Check your internet connection and try again later. Everything that is free stays available in the meantime.

Refunds

Apple handles refunds and decides on eligibility. A grown-up can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or read Apple’s refund instructions. We cannot issue refunds ourselves, and we never see your payment details.

The parent gate and the grown-ups’ area

Grown-up settings live in Family space. To open it:

  1. Go to Adventure or Treasures.
  2. Tap the round explorer picture with the small gear at the top left.
  3. Solve the multiplication question.

The parent gate asks a different multiplication of two two-digit numbers each time. It protects settings, profiles, backups, reminders, purchases and every link out of the app.

The parent gate is a practical safeguard that keeps young children out of grown-up areas. It is not age verification or legal parental consent.

Learner profiles

Each learner has their own missions, saved words and progress. A nickname is enough, and it is optional: without one, Qana uses “Explorer”. Please don’t use a child’s full name if a nickname will do.

Without Qana Family there is one learner profile. With Qana Family you can add up to six profiles on the device. Add, rename, switch or remove profiles in Family space.

Backups

Progress stays on the device; Qana does not sync it to a server. To keep a copy or move learning to another device:

  • Export a backup in Family space. This saves a file to Files, or wherever you choose.
  • Restore a backup in Family space on the other device and choose that file. Restoring replaces all current learner profiles and preferences on that device, so export first if you want to keep what is there.

Your device’s iCloud Backup or computer backups may also include Qana’s data, according to your Apple settings. Please never send us a backup file or post one publicly. How backups affect privacy.

Deleting data

In Family space, a grown-up can choose:

  • Remove a profile: deletes that learner’s progress, stars and stickers from the device, including from Qana’s internal recovery copy.
  • Erase all Qana data: deletes every learner, all progress and all settings on the device, and Qana starts over. A Qana Family purchase stays with your Apple Account (use Restore purchases). Exported backups in Files are not touched.

Both actions can’t be undone. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from that device. Exported backups and iCloud or computer device backups are separate copies; delete them in Files or your Apple settings. More about deleting data.

Reminders

Reminders are optional and off by default. Only a grown-up can turn them on, in Family space, and choose the time. A reminder is a quiet notification with the word of the day; it never shows a child’s name, and it is scheduled on the device.

If reminders don’t appear, check that notifications for Qana are allowed in your device’s Settings and that a Focus mode isn’t hiding them.

Widgets

Qana has two widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen: Word of the day and Letter of the day. They show course content that comes with the app and cannot read learner names or progress. To add one to the Home Screen, touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Qana.

Using Qana offline

Qana works without an internet connection, including the stories and their recordings. It makes no network requests of its own. You only need a connection to buy or restore Qana Family through the App Store.

Hjelp på norsk

Hva er Qana? Qana: Tigrinja for barn er laget for barn fra 6 til 8 år; yngre barn kan spille sammen med en voksen. Qana finnes for iPhone og iPad med iOS 18 eller nyere. Appen er på norsk eller engelsk, og det er tigrinja barna lærer.

Fem deler: Eventyr (8 verdener, 32 korte oppdrag med tre ord eller uttrykk hver), Spill (Ordpar, Orddetektiv, Boblepop og Mat fuglen med fem nivåer hver, og Lytt og finn), Bokstaver (alle de 32 bokstavfamiliene i fidel, med sporing og bokstavquiz), Historier (tre bildebøker) og Skatter.

Hva hører barnet? Bildebøkene og noen eksempelsetninger bruker publiserte opptak fra LIDA Stories, lest inn av en ekte forteller. Oppgavene kan leses opp på norsk eller engelsk med enhetens innebygde stemme. Myke lydeffekter kan slås av. Enkeltord og bokstaver har ikke opptak. Qana bruker aldri mikrofonen og vurderer ikke uttale.

Gratis og Qana Family: De to første verdenene, alle bokstaver og all sporing, alle spill og alle tre historier er gratis. Qana Family er et engangskjøp, ikke et abonnement, som åpner de seks andre verdenene og gir plass til opptil seks elevprofiler på enheten. Familiedeling støttes. Apple viser prisen før du kjøper.

Familieområdet: Trykk på det runde utforskerbildet med det lille tannhjulet øverst til venstre i Eventyr eller Skatter, og løs gangestykket. Her kjøper du Qana Family («Se Qana Family»), gjenoppretter kjøp («Gjenopprett kjøp»), styrer profiler og påminnelser og lager sikkerhetskopier. Foreldreporten er en praktisk sperre, ikke alderskontroll eller juridisk samtykke.

Sikkerhetskopi og sletting: Fremgangen lagres bare på enheten. «Eksporter sikkerhetskopi» lagrer en fil i Filer; «Gjenopprett sikkerhetskopi» erstatter alle profiler og innstillinger på enheten. «Fjern en profil» sletter den elevens fremgang. «Slett alle Qana-data» sletter alle elever, all fremgang og alle innstillinger; kjøpet følger Apple-kontoen, og eksporterte sikkerhetskopier berøres ikke. Sletter du appen, forsvinner også dataene.

Påminnelser og widgeter: Påminnelser er av som standard, og bare en voksen kan slå dem på. De viser dagens ord, aldri barnets navn. Widgetene Dagens ord og Dagens bokstav viser bare innhold som følger med appen. Qana fungerer uten nett; du trenger bare nett for å kjøpe eller gjenopprette.

Refusjon håndteres av Apple. Kontakt: samueltekie57@gmail.com. Vi svarer vanligvis innen 3 virkedager. Ikke send opplysninger om barnet, sikkerhetskopier eller betalingsinformasjon.
